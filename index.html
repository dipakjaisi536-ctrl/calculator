Important NEB Grading Rules
  • Theory Minimum: You must score at least 26.25 / 75 marks (or 17.5 / 50 marks) in theory exam.
  • Practical Minimum: You must score at least 8.75 / 25 marks (or 17.5 / 50 marks) in internal/practical exam.
  • Non-Graded (NG): Scoring below 35% in either Theory or Practical results in NG (Grade Point 0.0) for that subject. Students with NG in up to 2 subjects can appear in the immediate supplementary exam.
  • GPA Formula: $\text{GPA} = \frac{\sum (\text{Credit Hours} \times \text{Subject Grade Point})}{\text{Total Credit Hours}}$.
